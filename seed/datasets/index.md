# Datasets

* [Human-1M](human-1m.md) - The largest and most diverse high-quality egocentric dataset for physical AI, containing 1M hours of human demonstrations.
* [Multimodal Knowledge Agent](multimodal-knowledge-agent.md) - A benchmark dataset for evaluating autonomous AI agents on complex, multi-step, real-world visual knowledge work across professional domains.
* [PhysicalAI-Human-1M-100K](physicalai-human-1m-100k.md) - A large-scale egocentric video dataset with dense language annotations, head and hand motion, designed for training physical AI models.
* [Pixel Art Atlas](pixel-art-atlas.md) - A dataset of 1.4 million synthetic pixel art sprites with English captions, generated using Krea 2 Turbo and a pixel art LoRA.
* [Uzbek language corpus](uzbek-language-corpus.md) - A cleaned, annotated corpus of 5.6 million documents and 6 billion words in Uzbek, used for language model pretraining.
