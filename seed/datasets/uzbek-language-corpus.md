A cleaned, annotated corpus of 5.6 million documents and 6 billion words in Uzbek, used for language model pretraining.

# Principles

* Corpora for language model pretraining can be built from diverse, mixed-source data including scanned documents, born-digital texts, and web content.[^SRC-KB-0018] _(unreviewed, high confidence, observation)_
* Corpora can be annotated with metadata such as domain, topic, and register to support downstream tasks like model fine-tuning or research.[^SRC-KB-0018] _(unreviewed, high confidence, observation)_
* The quality of a corpus can be affected by the source material, with some sources (like books and archive documents) being over-represented.[^SRC-KB-0018] _(unreviewed, moderate confidence, claim)_
* The corpus is designed for pretraining and fine-tuning of language models for Uzbek, not for fact-checking or legal verification.[^SRC-KB-0018] _(unreviewed, moderate confidence, claim)_
