Corey Ganim and Spencer Kinney discuss AI adoption, business evaluation, and future trends, emphasizing practical ROI over hype. Corey proposes a three-part test for AI value: revenue growth, time savings, or quality improvement. They also cover content-driven audience building, leveraging social platforms like X, and transitioning from e-commerce to AI consulting using transferable business skills.

# Core method

To evaluate AI’s business value, apply Corey’s three-part test: does it increase revenue, save time, or improve product/service quality? Use social platforms like X to test ideas quickly and iteratively, then scale content to other formats. Leverage existing business skills (marketing, sales, hiring) to transition into AI consulting or building an AI-enabled ecosystem.

# Key takeaways

* Use Corey’s three-part test (money, time, quality) to evaluate AI’s business value.
* Leverage X (formerly Twitter) to test ideas quickly and scale content to other platforms.
* College is increasingly irrelevant for non-professional roles; practical skills and content creation matter more.
* Business fundamentals from e-commerce (marketing, hiring, partnerships) transfer directly to AI consulting.
* Cash flow and real estate remain strong for wealth-building, even in AI-driven economies.
* Voice AI and personal assistants will automate mundane tasks, freeing humans for higher-value work.
