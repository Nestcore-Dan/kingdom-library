# Concepts

* [Agency](agency.md) - A business entity that builds and deploys AI agents for clients, often monetizing through managed services.
* [Agent architecture](agent-architecture.md) - The design and structure of AI agent systems, including components and interactions.
* [Agent Management](agent-management.md) - The process of deploying, monitoring, and maintaining AI agents for clients or internal use.
* [Agent Operators](agent-operators.md) - A company associated with Nick Vasilescu and the development of AI agent collaboration tools, including Tango and Hermes Agent.
* [Agent orchestration](agent-orchestration.md) - The process of coordinating multiple AI agents to perform tasks in sequence or parallel.
* [Agent-to-Agent Communication](agent-to-agent-communication.md) - A mechanism allowing AI agents to coordinate or trigger actions without human intervention.
* [AI](ai.md) - Artificial Intelligence, a broad field encompassing machine learning, automation, and agent technologies.
* [AI Agent Swarm](ai-agent-swarm.md) - A method of deploying multiple AI agents to collaboratively reverse-engineer and rebuild software platforms.
* [AI agents](agent.md) - An autonomous software entity capable of performing tasks, often part of a multi-agent system.
* [AI consulting](ai-consulting.md) - The practice of advising businesses on AI adoption, implementation, and optimization.
* [Amazon FBA](amazon-fba.md) - A fulfillment service by Amazon for e-commerce sellers to manage inventory and shipping.
* [APIs](apis.md) - Application Programming Interfaces used to connect AI agents to messaging platforms and services.
* [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](bls-occupational-outlook-handbook.md) - A U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics resource used to classify human tasks in the Human-1M dataset.
* [Browser & computer use](browser-computer-use.md) - The field of AI agents that interact with and control computer systems, including web browsers and desktop applications.
* [business ecosystem](business-ecosystem.md) - A network of interconnected businesses, services, and technologies supporting a business model.
* [business fundamentals](business-fundamentals.md) - Core principles of business operations including marketing, sales, hiring, and partnerships.
* [Business Outcomes](business-outcomes.md) - The measurable results or goals a business seeks to achieve, which AI agents are designed to support and optimize.
* [Cash flow](real-estate.md) - The business of buying, selling, and managing property, often for wealth-building.
* [Circle of Influence](circle-of-influence.md) - A business strategy or agent workflow focused on leveraging personal networks and relationships for revenue generation.
* [Coding agents](coding-agents.md) - AI agents designed to write, edit, or configure code for automation or development tasks.
* [Cold Email Campaigns](cold-email-campaigns.md) - Automated outbound email sequences used for lead generation, often integrated with AI agents for personalization and QA.
* [College](college.md) - Formal education institutions, increasingly deemed less relevant for non-professional roles.
* [Computer Use Agents (CUA)](computer-use-agents-cua.md) - Agents that use LLMs to interact with computer environments through deterministic workflows.
* [CRM Integration](crm-integration.md) - The integration of AI agents with CRM systems to automate sales and customer workflows.
* [Deterministic Workflow Pipeline](deterministic-workflow-pipeline.md) - A structured, predictable sequence of steps (e.g., Python scripts) that agents execute to perform tasks.
* [Disney map](disney-map.md) - A metaphorical or literal reference to mapping or navigating business or AI landscapes.
* [Done for You Model](done-for-you-model.md) - A business model where operators spin up, deploy, and manage AI agents for clients without direct client interaction.
* [e-commerce business](e-commerce-business.md) - A business model centered on selling products or services online.
* [Editorial QA](editorial-qa.md) - A quality assurance loop within agent workflows to ensure output accuracy and business alignment before deployment.
* [Equity](equity.md) - A business model where revenue growth from deployed AI agents generates equity ownership for the agent operator.
* [Equity Partnerships](equity-partnerships.md) - A business model where operators earn equity ownership based on revenue growth generated by deployed AI agents.
* [Insurance Claims Processing](insurance-claims-processing.md) - An AI agent use case for automating insurance claim handling and workflow management.
* [LLM Agents](llm-agents.md) - Agents that use large language models to perform tasks, often in non-deterministic ways.
* [LLM tool integration](llm-tool-integration.md) - The practice of connecting LLMs to external tools or APIs to perform tasks.
* [Location Sharing](location-sharing.md) - A feature enabled through messaging platforms that allows AI agents to share geographic locations with users.
* [Low-code, no-code AI](low-code-no-code-ai.md) - Approach to building AI agent workflows using minimal or no programming, leveraging visual tools or simple scripting.
* [Markdown](markdown.md) - A lightweight markup language used to format text in Obsidian notes.
* [Marketing](marketing.md) - The practice of promoting and selling products or services, including digital and social media strategies.
* [Messaging interfaces](messaging-interfaces.md) - Interfaces for AI agents to communicate via text, email, or voice.
* [Minimal Viable Agent (MVA)](minimal-viable-agent-mva.md) - A concept for building the simplest possible agent to deliver value quickly.
* [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](model-context-protocol-mcp.md) - A protocol for connecting AI models to tools and interfaces, used in Paper MCP for UI reconstruction.
* [Moonshot](moonshot.md) - A high-risk, high-reward initiative or project, often in AI or technology.
* [Multi-agent systems](multi-agent-systems.md) - Systems composed of multiple AI agents working together to achieve goals.
* [OAuth](oauth.md) - An authentication protocol used for securing agent access to Tango’s task management system.
* [Personal assistant](personal-assistant.md) - An AI system designed to assist users with tasks, often through voice or text interfaces.
* [Phone Number](phone-number.md) - A real-world identifier assigned to AI agents to enable direct user interaction via messaging platforms.
* [Private investments](private-investments.md) - Investments made in private companies or assets, often for high returns.
* [Private Lending](private-lending.md) - A form of financing where individuals or entities lend money to others.
* [Reactions](reactions.md) - A messaging feature that allows users to respond to AI agents with emoji or reactions.
* [Referral Model](referral-model.md) - A business model where clients refer new customers to earn commissions or equity.
* [Robot companions](robot-companions.md) - AI-driven companions or assistants designed to interact with users.
* [ROI](roi.md) - Return on Investment, a metric for evaluating the profitability of business activities.
* [Sales Inquiry Triage](sales-inquiry-triage.md) - A business process where AI agents automatically categorize and route incoming sales inquiries.
* [Social Media](social-media.md) - Digital platforms for content sharing, audience engagement, and business promotion.
* [Writing](writing.md) - The act of producing text, often automated or assisted by AI tools.
