# Tango knowledge

Knowledge that belongs to [Tango](../tango.md).

* [Audit trails](audit-trails.md) - A record of actions taken by agents, used for accountability and traceability.
* [Hand off task](hand-off-task.md) - A mechanism to transfer task ownership between agents in a workflow.
* [Hash](hash.md) - A cryptographic function used by Tango to generate signed receipts for audit trails.
* [JSON](json.md) - A lightweight data-interchange format used by Tango to store task records and receipts.
* [Lease](lease.md) - A mechanism in Tango to assign task ownership to agents, preventing duplication and enabling failover for stalled tasks.
* [Project management](project-management.md) - The practice of planning, organizing, and managing resources to achieve specific goals.
* [Specialized agents](specialized-agents.md) - AI agents designed for specific tasks or domains, managed and coordinated through Tango’s task system.
* [Task decomposition](task-decomposition.md) - The process of breaking down a complex task into smaller, manageable subtasks.
* [Task leases](task-leases.md) - A mechanism to prevent duplicate work by assigning temporary ownership of tasks to agents.
* [Worker handle](worker-handle.md) - A unique identifier assigned to AI agents or workers to manage task assignment and ownership in Tango.
* [worker key](worker-key.md) - A credential used by agents to authenticate and access tasks in the Tango system.
