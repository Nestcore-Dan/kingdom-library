Justin Bellware shares how he helped scale an Australian startup from $30K to $700K/month using AI agents focused on revenue generation, emphasizing education-first onboarding, agent specialization, and business outcome alignment over tool sales. He details workflows like automated cold email campaigns with editorial QA and agent systems built around business priorities, not just AI capabilities. The approach centers on turning personal expertise into repeatable, productized agent workflows that clients can deploy and scale.

# Core method

Start by educating the client on AI tools before deploying agents, then build a revenue-focused agent (like a 'Revenue Partner') around their core business buckets (outbound, CRM, circle of influence, events). Use low-code/no-code tools to automate workflows (e.g., email campaigns with QA loops), and gradually add specialized agents as business needs evolve. Prioritize business outcomes over AI features, and productize your agent workflows for scalable client deployment.

# Key takeaways

* Build AI agents around clear business priorities (e.g., revenue, CRM, outreach) rather than generic AI capabilities.
* Use an education-first onboarding model to help clients understand how to use AI tools before deploying agents.
* Implement editorial QA loops in agent workflows (e.g., email campaigns) to ensure quality and reduce errors.
* Productize agent workflows into repeatable systems (e.g., 'Revenue Partner') that can be sold or scaled to multiple clients.
* Focus on business outcomes — not AI tool sales — to create durable, client-driven value.
* Use low-code/no-code tools (like Hermes, Python, Slack) to automate and iterate on workflows quickly.
