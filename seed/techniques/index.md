# Techniques

* [Agent productization](agent-productization.md) - The process of turning agent workflows into repeatable, scalable, and sellable products or services for clients.
* [cron](cron.md) - A scheduling technique used to automate periodic agent tasks.
* [Education-first onboarding](education-first-onboarding.md) - A client onboarding method that prioritizes teaching users how to use AI tools before deploying agents, ensuring adoption and alignment.
* [Voice AI](voice-ai.md) - AI systems that process or generate speech, including voice assistants and voice agents.
